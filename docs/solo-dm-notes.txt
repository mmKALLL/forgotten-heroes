Pillars of play:

Interaction:
Use 6d12 or DMG p.73 goal generator (D = dungeon / W = wilderness / O = other goals)
First three quest sources are “high rank” and give nicer rewards, but have requirements
Latter three sources are “low rank” and require 1 gp per quest generation
Roll 1d4 to find out which reward is given in addition to milestone XP
Roll 1d4 to find out quest length (-1 for low-rank): short / medium / long / epic
Biome is based on world map location
Can find quests from (d6 or roleplay):
Nobility like the king, counts, dukes, and barons (20% D / 20% W / 60% O)
Need: to increase realm standing or gain access to elusive people / items
Reward: magical item / masterwork equip / personal favor / campaign clue
Requirement: 8 renown or aristocratic lifestyle, 10 gp / day and up
Guilds or factions, based on each faction’s motivations (25% D / 25% W / 50% O)
Need: hamper / manipulate / ransack rivals, or gain access to people
Reward: hireling / skill proficiency / item mastery / guild-specific equipment
Requirement: 5 renown, membership in guild or wealthy lifestyle, 4 gp / day
City council, based on needs of the city’s leadership (60% D / 30% W / 10% O)
Need: to prevent threats to the stability of factions, guilds, or city safety
Reward: bounties for lots of money / renown, rarely anything else
Requirement: 3 renown or comfortable lifestyle, 2 gp / day
Barkeep, based on rumors (50% D / 50% W)
Need: visit a mysterious settlement, faction, person, or location
Reward: quest lead / objective with hoard / magical item / campaign clue
Requirement: 1 renown or 10 gp
Bar visitors like caravan traders, adventuring parties, mysterious figures, and common folk (75% D / 25% W)
Need: take care of things that threaten safety on the road
Reward: quest lead / low bounty / objective with hoard / campaign clue
Shopkeepers, craftsmen, and other individuals (25% D / 75% W)
Need: solve personal problems, acquire an uncommon item, etc.
Reward: based on shop type - discounts / favors / money / renown

Exploration:
Must rest a minimum of a week between adventures.
Modest lifestyle confers no penalty nor benefit, -1/+1 to proficiency bonus for next adventure per lifestyle tier decrease/increase
Travel 3 hexes (= 30 km) per day, strict rationing needed
Suffer an exhaustion level per day without rations
Successful Survival check (DC15) can prevent exhaustion
Short rest allows using Hit Die, long rest recovers HP and Hit Die
Long rest consumes healing kits for each PC whose HP is restored (1 gp each)
If you don’t have a healing kit when stabilizing, the roll is done at disadvantage
(Optional) First death is an inconvenience (failed quest and lost GP), second is a temporary injury (-4 to two random stats, heals 1 per week), third onwards is (randomly) a permanent loss of 1 class level or 2 random stat points. This is tracked separately for each character.

Dungeons:
Use a cool dungeon map; use solo adventurer’s guide or donjon for generating contents.
Use kobold fight club or GPT for encounters; set creature type and environment.
Use TSAT2 “entering rooms” (p.51) with story points or 6d12 for rooms
Only generate the BBEG once enough clues are found, based on story. DMG p.74
Inspiration points (green): receive 3 after each long rest or with great roleplaying
Inspiration is shared with the party
Can use 1 to reroll any failure or gain a spent hit die
Can use 2 to gain an extra action, negate an instance of incoming damage
Clue points (blue): receive 1 when you enter an empty room
2 needed for unlocking BBEG lair in short dungeon, 3 in medium and 4 in long
Collecting a multiple of the amount lets you choose one (= push your luck):
Gain knowledge about a secret weakness of the BBEG
Gain knowledge of a hidden magic item in BBEG hoard
You’re allowed to revisit old rooms; roll contents in each case
Alert points (red): scale between 0 and 9, represents how aggressive the dungeon is
0: Undetected, standard sneaking and surprise rules
For each point: -10 to surprise/ambush roll and +1 to enemy passive perception
4+: Sneaking and dungeon crawl combat AI are disabled, BBEG has traps
Get -2 from clearing a level
Get +1 from short rest or if an enemy shouts a warning
Get +2 from an alert trap or if a warning horn is blown
Get +3 if an enemy flees combat
Empty rooms allow a short rest
Empty floors allow a long rest - but with alert you need to roll a deadly random encounter

Combat:
No initiative order: everyone in player party moves first, then everyone in enemy party
Randomly choose between dungeon crawl event system (easy), combat AI (normal), and tactical positioning beatdown (hard). Can choose for an entire quest for less hassle.
Normal enemies get weaker as they’re damaged, incentivizing spreading of damage. (Also use morale for party dynamics.)
BBEGs get stronger or more desperate as they’re damaged, incentivizing epic setups.
Use kobold fight club to generate enemies, don't worry too much about theme for now
Roll d100 to determine surprise. reduce 10 per alert level, add 20 if whole party had successful stealth check against enemy passive perception
1-10: ambush with extra traps and tactical advantage
11-20: ambush with extra traps
21-35: enemy surprise turn
36-60: neutral combat start - only half of player party moves
61-80: player notices enemy first; parley or surprise from outside room
81-100: player sneaks on enemy: surprise from within room
Choose between ⅔ speed with diagonals or full speed with FE style.
Flanking rules: 8-way combat only confers advantage from behind, 4-way FE style provides advantage for both flankers (since AOO take more manouvering to avoid)
Prioritize actions that might make for a better story over strictly following the combat AI.

XP gain:
¼ of standard combat exp is awarded
¼ of level exp gained for every 2 story points
(multi-level) descending a floor, (single-level) midway point, defeating a midboss
Approx. 2 in a short dungeon, 3 in a mid, 4 in a long, 5+ in an epic
¼ of level exp gained as quest reward (divided equally among main objectives)
¼ of level exp gained from optional high-risk treasure / objectives
