# Balance constants

The engine's balance and tuning numbers are all in **[`src/engine/constants.ts`](../../src/engine/constants.ts)**, grouped by topic: starting party, travel and encounters, pace, pursuit, foraging, supplies and rest (`CAMP_HEAL`, a day in camp or in town without a room; `COMMON_ROOM_HEAL` and `PRIVATE_ROOM_HEAL`, a night in an inn's rooms), carrying capacity, markets, geographic supply, the price book, services and animals, visible wealth, stowing away, politics, settlements, notable people, reputation, rumours, contracts, recruits (the party cap, sign-on fees, rolled stats, old wounds and starting gear), camaraderie (its losses and gains, parting and the low spell), points of interest, site cycles and boons, exploring, gathering, rule hooks and accessories, Strain, checks (including `CHALLENGE_MARKS`, what each tier marks on a skill challenge's tally, and `DOWNED_EXHAUSTION`, the exhaustion a wound costs a member it drops to 0 HP), territory, progression (the XP table and what pays XP), challenge ratings (the CR → DC table, `CR_STANDARD`, the CR content is written at, and `CR_WOUND_STEP`, how much deeper wounds go per CR above it, and `WOUND_REFERENCE_HP`, the max HP written wounds were tuned for: a party with a higher average takes them that much deeper, or by the CR's factor when larger), levelled work (contract levels and pay by level), authored dungeons (how many are placed, the near-start rule, lairs' CR), delving (wounds, the hoard, and the dungeon and lair XP: `DUNGEON_XP_SHARE`, `DUNGEON_MILESTONE_SHARE`, `DUNGEON_END_SHARE`, `LAIR_XP_SHARE`, shares of what the level of its CR needs to level up), world danger (the ramp), and the Crisis (rolling it, the calendar, the danger surge, the waiting penalty, clues and thwarting, and rumours, the summons and omens). Each has a one-line comment, and each section names the module that uses it. Party upgrades (Plan 6c) are their own section: what each level of the camp kit, the smuggler's compartment, the surveyor's maps, delving gear, the company banner and the scout's kit does (`CAMP_KIT_HEAL`, the share of max HP a camp rest heals more per level, `SMUGGLE_SHARE`, `MAPS_EXPLORE`, `DELVING_GEAR_BONUS`, `DELVING_GEAR_SKILLS`, `DELVING_GEAR_CHALLENGE_LEVEL`, `BANNER_SOFTEN`, `SCOUT_CHECK_BONUS` on surprise and Exposed checks, `SCOUT_HOSTILE_PER_LEVEL` off the road's hostile share); their prices are data (below). Permanent purchases: waterskins are `WATERSKIN_PRICE` and `WATERSKIN_SELL_PRICE` (supplies), animals are priced in `data/claude/animals.json` (below); the courier's news is `COURIER_NEIGHBOURS_PRICE` (every neighbouring town) or `COURIER_ALL_PRICE` (every town, its news `COURIER_ALL_SPEED` × as fast), a day older per `COURIER_KM_PER_DAY` km (services and animals); the market's Elsewhere column ignores price-book entries older than `ELSEWHERE_MAX_AGE_DAYS` (the price book). Edit a value there, then run `pnpm test`. Gathering takes `GATHER_DAYS` (2) days, and `GATHERER_RATIONS` is what the gatherer eats a day (the rest of the party waits).

**Watch out:** some tests pin exact values (for example `ENCOUNTER_SLOT_CHANCE`, `BOON_COOLDOWN_DAYS`) or measure statistics such as the Rampant share and POIs per world. When you deliberately change a number, update the matching test expectation.

## Knobs in the data files

| File | Field | What it controls |
|---|---|---|
| `data/claude/threats.json` | each threat's `strength` | How strong regional threats are (moves the Rampant share) |
| `data/procedural/poi-types.json` | `weight`, `threat`, `water` per type | How common each POI type is, its threat and whether it has water |
| `data/original/skills.json` | each skill's `ability` | The default ability for each skill |
| `data/claude/goods.json` | `weight` (kg), `price` (gp), `depth` per good | What cargo weighs against 20 kg per member at STR 10, its base price (nothing spoils), and how many units a market absorbs before bulk trades move the price |
| `data/claude/producers.json` | each rule's `when` and `output` (1–3 per good) | Where goods are made: land, coast, type, size, wealth, the sites in the domain and its services (`services`). A rule with `supplyAtLeast` makes a worked good only where its inputs are to hand (e.g. forges make arms and armour where metal's supply is 1 or more), judged on the supply of the rules without inputs. A market's supply is the best producer within reach, a level less per `SUPPLY_DAYS_PER_LEVEL` steady days, down to `SUPPLY_MIN` |
| `data/claude/market-rules.json` | each rule's `when`, `label` and `demand` (−2..2) | Which settlements want a good: land, coast, type, the type's focus (attack, defence), size, wealth, threat level (`threatAtLeast`, `threatAtMost`) and nearby sites. Demand sums and clamps to −3..3; demand − supply (within ±3) moves the price ×1.25 a level. The label is shown when the market explains a price |
| `data/claude/service-roles.json` | `trades`, `buysOnly`, `actions` per service | Which shops buy and sell which goods, and what each service offers (none means "coming later") |
| `data/claude/service-roles.json` | `inputs` per service (1–3 per good) | The goods a service's work uses: each adds that much demand wherever the service is (a smith wants metal, a healer healing), summed with the market rules. Store investments will scale it later |
| `data/original/settlement-types.json` | `focus` per type (`attack`, `defence`, `other`) | What a settlement is built for, for the market rules: camps and forts attack (arms), cities and capitals defend (armour) |
| `data/claude/animals.json` | `price`, `hirePerDay`, `capacity` (kg), `speed`, `waterPerDay`, `maxPathDanger`, `vehicle`, `slow`, `flies`, `desertWater`, `soldIn` | Pack and riding animals and vehicles: what they cost (the price is the whole cost: no upkeep, designer call 2026-11-13), carry and drink, and where they can go; which add `VEHICLE_HOSTILE` (vehicle), rule out brisk and forced (slow: carts, wagons, the donkey), fly (the carpet), save desert water (camels: `CAMEL_DESERT_WATER_SAVING` each, up to `CAMEL_DESERT_WATER_MAX`) and where they are sold |
| `data/claude/upgrades.json` | `basePrice` and `soldBy` per upgrade | What each party upgrade costs (level n costs `basePrice` × n gp: 75 the camp kit, 100 the maps and the delving gear, 125 the smuggler's compartment, 150 the scout's kit, 200 the company banner) and which services sell it |
| `data/claude/person-roles.json` | `weight`, `minSize`, `service` per role | Which notable people a settlement has |
| `data/claude/service-roles.json` | `recruits`: `offers`, `levels`, `picks`, `classes`, `fee` per service | Who hires out (the inn, the adventurers' guild, mercenaries): how many recruits a week, their levels (drawn in this range, 0 is a level-1 recruit with two old wounds; those above the lowest living member's level + 2 are listed but won't join), their quality (`picks`: the best of n sets of 3d6 rolls), classes, and a multiplier on the sign-on fee |
| `data/claude/contracts.json` | `km`, `amount`, `passengers`, `urgentChance`, `pay`, `reputation`, `minSize`, `minStanding` per template | Notice-board contracts: how far they send the party, how much cargo, how often they are urgent, the pay multiplier and the reputation they earn |
| `data/claude/poi-situations.json` | precious `goods` amounts in lair-leader and vault outcomes | How much dungeon and lair loot comes as relics, gems and luxuries rather than gold |
| `data/claude/service-roles.json` | `accessories.offers` and `accessories.maxTier` (Magic Items, finesmith) | How many accessories a shop offers a week and the best tier it sells |
| `data/procedural/accessories.json` | `weight`, `minTier`, `value`, `bases` and the hook's amount per part | How often each base and affix is rolled, which tier it needs, what it adds to the price, which bases it fits and how strong its rule hook is |
| `data/claude/poi-situations.json` | `accessory` effects (and their `tier`) in lair and dungeon clearings | Where accessories drop as loot |
| `data/claude/gathering.json` | `biomes`, `skill`, `dc` and `yield` per tier per rule | What one member can gather in each land over a gathering (`GATHER_DAYS` days), how hard it is (before the domain's producers lower the DC) and how many units each tier brings home |
| `data/claude/villains.json` | `weight`, `threats` and `boss` per villain; `biomes`, `seat`, `leadCr`, `keyCr` and `surge` per act | How often each villain is rolled for a world (favoured where its threats hold a land), where each key site is placed, how hard each act's lead and key encounters are (their DCs come from the CR), and whether the danger surges before the act is due |
| `data/claude/dungeons-1.json`, `dungeons-2.json` | `cr`, `biomes` and `hoard` (`precious`, `accessory`) per dungeon; `milestone` per room | Each authored dungeon's difficulty (its rooms' DCs move by `crDc(cr) − crDc(CR_STANDARD)` and their wounds by `crWound(cr)`), where it may be placed, the goods and accessories its hoard holds besides the gold (`DELVE_REWARD` × a same-level contract's pay), and its midway rooms, which pay `DUNGEON_MILESTONE_SHARE` of its XP once per clearing |
| `data/claude/dungeon-rooms-1.json`, `dungeon-rooms-2.json` | each room situation's `dc`s and wounds, written at standard difficulty | How hard each room is before its dungeon's CR, and how deep its wounds go before `DELVE_WOUND` |
| `data/claude/dungeon-sites.json` | `pathDanger` of the `authored-dungeon` type | How dangerous the path to every authored dungeon is |
| `data/claude/crisis-sites.json` | `pathDanger` and `water` per type | How dangerous the path to each Crisis site is, and whether it refills the waterskins (every one does: some lie days off the road in the desert) |
| `data/claude/crisis-situations.json`, `dungeon-rooms-1.json`, `dungeon-rooms-2.json` | each finale's or boss room's `challenge` `successes` and `failures`, and its round options | How long a climax's skill challenge is (by CR: 3/2, 4/3, 5/3) and what each round costs |
| `data/claude/crisis-situations.json` | the `dc` of each Crisis situation's checks (read as offsets from 12, `CRISIS_DC_BASE`), the wounds (`hp`, played as written, so written for the act's CR), the gold paid and asked, and `xp` 0 on the approach checks | How much harder or easier each check is than its act's CR, how much a visit hurts and pays, and that only the finale (and a lead's last stage) pays XP |
| `data/claude/situations.json`, `road-situations-2.json`, `poi-situations.json`, `archetypes.json`, the Crisis omens in `crisis-situations.json`, `data/procedural/twists.json` | the negative `hp` effects (wounds) | How much a road or site encounter hurts, as written for a novice party (designer call 2026-11-14, design §7): one member 4–6 low stakes, 7–10 moderate, 11–15 serious; everyone (`all`) about 60% of that (3–4, 5–6, 7–9); nothing below 3. Partial lighter than fail, fail than critical failure. Dungeon rooms and Crisis sites have their own scale (above) |

Encounter odds and rewards live in the situations themselves (`situations.json` files in every source). The fixed wounds outside situations are in the same band: `GATHER_INJURY` (6) and `STOWAWAY_BRAWL_DAMAGE` (5–10).

See also [threat-levels.md](threat-levels.md) for how threat and Rampant are worked out.

The economy's numbers (spreads, market depth, contract pay and deadlines, goods prices) were tuned against a simulation: see [economy-report.md](economy-report.md). Rerun it after changing them:

```
ECONOMY_REPORT=/tmp/economy.md pnpm vitest run src/content/economy.sim.test.ts
```

The simulation starts its parties with its own 50 gp (`SIM_GOLD` in the test), so its guards and tables don't move with `STARTING_GOLD`; the report ends with a reference section for the game's own `STARTING_GOLD` start.

The Crisis numbers (the `CRISIS_*` constants, `XP_THWART`, and the act DCs), and since Plan 6 Track B the camaraderie constants, `XP_BY_LEVEL`, `CR_DC`, `DOWNED_EXHAUSTION`, `CR_WOUND_STEP` and the dungeon XP shares, were checked against the Crisis simulation: see [crisis-report.md](crisis-report.md). Rerun it after changing them:

```
CRISIS_REPORT=/tmp/crisis.md pnpm vitest run src/content/crisis.sim.test.ts
```

`CRISIS_ACT_DAYS` (about 100 days an act) is the designer's number; the report says what changing it would do instead of tuning it.
